# Pixel dojo gameplay

Gameplay uses the supplied dojo backdrop with generated painterly wooden frames, hanji panels and ivory striking tiles. See [generation prompts and asset paths](dojo-generated-assets.md). The backdrop is an overlay canvas without a GraphicRaycaster. GameplayViewport renders the world camera to a screen-sized render texture and displays only the cropped region inside the central frame, inset at its sides and bottom, with its top cropped at 80% of screen height so the lanes enter from the sky below the ceiling. The central window is transparent and has no scroll or frame graphic. Lanes and notes cannot draw over the frame or surrounding scenery. The original screen projection is preserved so judgment effects remain aligned. The texture follows canvas resolution changes and is released when the viewport is disabled or unloaded.

The authoritative GameplayHudLayoutBuilder calls PixelGameplayStyle.Apply. The song and judgment-count cards use paper and dark ink; combo, health and recent judgment use a dark wooden panel with warm cream, sage, amber and red feedback. Gameplay labels, headings, results and lane keycaps use Mona12TextKR. All lanes and notes now share the same ivory palette; per-lane note tinting is disabled. Existing chart, camera projection, timing, input, health and restart behavior remain intact.

Lane key indicators use the generated striking-tile sprite and dark labels. Held and judgment flashes remain state-dependent. **Game Tools > Art > Prepare Generated Gameplay Assets** imports the generated PNGs without power-of-two resizing, creates cropped nine-slice sprites and connects the note texture. Regenerate Gameplay through **Game Tools > Scenes > Build Gameplay** after changing its builder. Scene generation only reads these prepared assets.

Gameplay also contains an untagged display camera with a zero culling mask and no AudioListener. It keeps the screen output active while the Main Camera renders into the viewport texture, preventing Unity's "No cameras rendering" message without drawing world geometry outside the frame. The MainCamera tag and its AudioListener remain on the world camera for input and hit-feedback projection.
