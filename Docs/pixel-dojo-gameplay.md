# Pixel dojo gameplay

The lane spans 97.5% of the Gameplay screen, with a 1.5% margin at the top and 1% at the bottom. The judgment line sits 8.3% above the bottom, giving incoming notes 90.2% of the screen height before contact. The combo, grade and timing appear together near the upper center of the lane. The judgment-position setting moves them together, and passing notes draw in front of the text. These 1920×1080 Game View captures show the same hold and 64-hit combo. [Previous layout](verification-images/gameplay-lane-feedback-before.jpg) · [Current layout](verification-images/gameplay-lane-feedback.jpg)

An ivory tablet with a vermilion seal marks a tap. A jade clasp, woven band and separate end cap mark a hold. A thin ivory rule crosses the receptors. Beat ticks mark the lane edges and a dotted line crosses the field every four beats. Press Esc during play to adjust **레인 불투명도** with the mouse or the left and right arrow keys. The slider fades the field, dividers and timing grid and saves the value for the next song. The earlier layout study shows why the field keeps a constant width from entry to contact. [Layout study](verification-images/arcade-lane-layouts.jpg) · [Earlier hold sequence](verification-images/arcade-lane-hold-sequence.jpg)

The stage's orthographic camera renders scenery layer 30. The screen-space overlay draws the masked field and HUD. A lane is 32 drawing units wide and the field is 240 units high. Gameplay and chart preview fit the height to the available rectangle and limit the width by lane count. The judgment line sits 18 units above the bottom. Each unit from `IScrollTimeline.DistanceBetween` moves the head ten drawing units above that line. At the default eight scroll units per second, heads enter about 2.78 seconds before contact. A held head stays on the line while its end follows the chart. Beat markers, receptor flashes and judgment effects use the same coordinates.

The normal song flow and the six-key fixture use the same field. [Earlier layout study across screen sizes and lane counts](verification-images/lane-refinement-aspects.jpg)

The field and note artwork is authored in `Tools/Art/ArcadeField.svg`. Run `python Tools/bake_lane_art.py` with `resvg-py` installed to bake the PNG sprites in `Assets/Game/Art/ArcadeField/`. They use eight texels per drawing unit and bilinear filtering. **Game Tools > Scenes > Build Gameplay** prepares the authored templates and default field. **Build Chart Editor** regenerates the embedded preview. Set `RHYTHM_DOJO_CAPTURE_LANES=1` in the Editor process and run `DojoBackgroundFlowTests` to save stage and pattern captures under `Logs/action-stages/frames/`.
